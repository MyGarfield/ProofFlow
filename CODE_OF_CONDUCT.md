# Code of Conduct

ProofFlow contributors must make technical disagreement safe, specific and evidence-driven.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect across experience, identity, language and background.
- Critique claims, code, tests and evidence rather than the person presenting them.
- State uncertainty, correct errors openly and distinguish verified facts from hypotheses.
- Protect confidential reports, personal data, credentials and restricted customer material.
- Give reviewers enough context to reproduce a result without demanding access to private systems.

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment, threats, discrimination or sexualized attention;
- publishing personal information, secrets or private reports without permission;
- impersonation, deceptive evidence, fabricated test results or deliberate claim escalation;
- retaliation against good-faith reporters or reviewers;
- using project spaces primarily for spam, promotion or unrelated disputes.

## Enforcement

For conduct that can be discussed safely, open a concise repository issue. For confidential conduct or safety
concerns, use [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/MyGarfield/ProofFlow/security/advisories/new)
and prefix the title with `CODE OF CONDUCT`. The maintainer may edit or remove content, restrict participation,
or permanently ban accounts when necessary. Decisions should document the applicable rule without exposing
the reporter or restricted evidence.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, release channels and project events.
